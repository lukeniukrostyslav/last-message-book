# TIMELINE

Status: LOCKED WORKING CANON

## Day -6 years
Alexei works on a confidential predictive-risk project. A serious incident occurs. Key records are compartmentalised. Alexei leaves the project and suppresses/loses access to critical details.

## Day -30
A scheduled archive-protection protocol is activated after detecting an impending deletion event.

## Day 0
Alexei receives:
> «У тебя осталось 30 дней. Не доверяй человеку, который придёт первым.»

Later the same day he receives a photograph apparently dated Day +1 showing himself beside Elena Varga.

## Days 1–7
Alexei verifies the message, investigates the photograph, contacts Daniel, and discovers links to his old project.

## Days 8–15
Evidence indicates that the project predicted and manipulated human behaviour. Alexei learns Elena has been investigating the same archive.

## Days 16–22
The anonymous system sends increasingly specific evidence. Alexei's own role becomes suspect. Sofia learns he has concealed important parts of his past.

## Days 23–27
Alexei and Elena locate the archive's physical/technical redundancy. Former insiders begin destroying evidence and pressuring witnesses.

## Days 28–29
Alexei discovers the 30-day deadline is not a prediction of his death. It is the final scheduled deletion window for the archive and the last point at which its integrity can be established.

## Day 30
The deletion event begins. Alexei must choose between preserving evidence, protecting people close to him, and exposing his own role. The archive survives in a verifiable form, but the consequences become public.

## Future-evidence rule
No supernatural time travel. The "tomorrow" photograph is a manufactured future-looking artifact: controlled staging + delayed release + manipulated metadata. Its purpose is psychological pressure.
