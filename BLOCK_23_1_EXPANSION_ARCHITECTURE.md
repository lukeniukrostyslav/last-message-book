Date: 2026-09-19

## Purpose
Define the controlled architecture for expanding the current 26,890-word manuscript toward the 70,000–100,000-word commercial target without padding, canon drift, duplicate clues, or artificial length.

## Locked baseline
- Current verified manuscript: **26,890 words**.
- Minimum target: **70,000 words**.
- Upper planning target: **100,000 words**.
- Current gap to 70k: **43,110 words**.
- Current gap to 100k: **73,110 words**.
- These gaps are planning measurements, not quotas.

## Expansion principles
1. Add scenes because the story needs them, not because a chapter needs a word quota.
2. Every new scene must materially add character, evidence, suspense, consequence, relationship change, thematic depth, atmosphere, or decision pressure.
3. No new supernatural mechanism.
4. M remains an automated protocol using archived data, schedules, behavioural models, staged evidence and delayed releases.
5. The future-looking photograph remains manufactured.
6. Alexei retains agency; M never becomes the author of human choices.
7. No major reveal may be moved earlier merely to create more pages.
8. New clues require a purpose and a later payoff or controlled ambiguity.
9. New scenes must connect causally to the surrounding chapters.
10. Existing finished material is not rewritten unless the new expansion creates a concrete continuity, pacing, canon or repetition defect.
11. Work proceeds in manuscript order.
12. Every completed production checkpoint is saved to GitHub.

## Expansion architecture by story function

### ACT I — WARNING / INVESTIGATION
**Prologue + Chapters 01–10**

Primary expansion goals:
- establish Alexei's normal professional life and forensic method before the mystery overwhelms it;
- deepen the emotional and practical impact of the 30-day warning;
- expand the investigation of the photograph and its provenance;
- give Elena and Daniel more scene-level presence rather than exposition-only functions;
- show the first behavioural steering attempts by M;
- deepen the six-year PR-06 history without prematurely explaining Alexei's full role;
- strengthen cause-and-effect between Alexei's choices and the next evidence discovery.

Protected mysteries:
- who initiated M;
- how the photograph was staged;
- what exactly Alexei did six years earlier;
- whether Elena can be trusted;
- the full meaning of the 30-day clock.

### ACT II-A — PROJECT / M / MEMORY
**Chapters 11–20**

Primary expansion goals:
- make the old predictive-risk project feel like a lived system with people, decisions and consequences;
- deepen Daniel's verification work through concrete investigative scenes;
- deepen Sofia's relationship with Alexei and the cost of his secrecy;
- expand Elena's investigation and independent evidence trail;
- show how M can steer behaviour without future knowledge;
- build the distinction between evidence and interpretation through action;
- increase the psychological pressure around Alexei's unreliable memory;
- make the photograph revelation emotionally consequential, not merely technical.

Protected mysteries:
- exact scope of Alexei's responsibility;
- identity/function of the missing modelling layer;
- Hale's full involvement;
- the buried incident.

### ACT II-B — RESPONSIBILITY / CONVERGENCE
**Chapters 21–30**

Primary expansion goals:
- progressively reveal Alexei's responsibility instead of delivering it in a single exposition block;
- deepen the buried incident and its human consequences;
- give Mark Reid and Thomas Rain distinct, bounded witness functions;
- increase Hale's pressure through choices and consequences rather than generic threats;
- deepen Sofia's discovery and response;
- make the reserve archive and evidence chain operationally credible;
- force Alexei to choose accountability before Day 30;
- increase the cost of preserving the evidence.

Protected mysteries:
- exact history of the incident;
- how much Hale controlled;
- how much Alexei knowingly contributed;
- M's final purpose.

### ACT III / IV — DELETION / ACCOUNTABILITY / AFTERMATH
**Chapters 31–40**

Primary expansion goals:
- make Days 28–30 feel consequential rather than compressed;
- show the evidence-release preparation through concrete actions;
- give Elena, Daniel and Sofia distinct roles;
- deepen Alexei's decision to become an accountable witness;
- show the deletion event without turning it into an action-movie sequence;
- give the public release meaningful immediate consequences;
- deepen the personal and professional cost in the aftermath;
- preserve the restrained 08:16 ending and Alexei's final choice.

Protected ending:
- Book 1 resolves the archive, deadline, M mechanism, photograph mechanism and Alexei's role.
- The final timestamp remains a controlled sequel thread.
- No new material may make the ending depend on supernatural time travel.
