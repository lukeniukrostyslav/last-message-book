# CHARACTERS

## Protagonist — Alexei Morozov
Age: 38
Occupation: digital-forensics specialist working independently on corporate incident investigations.
Public image: methodical, quiet, dependable.
Core fear: discovering that his own memory and decisions cannot be trusted.
Core desire: restore control over his life after a personal loss.
Secret: years earlier, Alexei participated in a confidential predictive-risk project and later convinced himself he had forgotten its most important details.
Arc: from believing evidence is objective to understanding that evidence can be engineered — and that he must take responsibility for choices he once tried to bury.

## Person in the future photograph — Elena Varga
Age: 36
Occupation: investigative journalist specialising in technology and corporate misconduct.
Connection to Alexei: they crossed paths years earlier through the predictive-risk project, but Alexei does not initially remember the extent of it.
Public identity: independent journalist.
Actual role: she has been independently investigating the project and possesses evidence that can expose what happened.

## Message sender — "M"
Identity: initially unknown.
Actual mechanism: an automated communication system built from archived project data, scheduled triggers and human-authored instructions; it is not supernatural time travel.
Motivation: force Alexei to reach evidence before a coordinated deletion event occurs on Day 30.
Important limitation: the system cannot know arbitrary future events. The apparent future photograph is produced through a controlled setup and metadata manipulation designed to make Alexei believe it was taken tomorrow.

## Antagonistic force
A private technology contractor and several former project insiders who want the archive destroyed before Day 30.
The antagonist is a network of people protecting the project, not a single cartoon villain.

## Supporting cast
### Sofia Morozova
Alexei's younger sister. Emotional anchor and the person most capable of challenging his self-narrative.

### Daniel Weiss
Cybersecurity investigator and Alexei's former colleague. Practical ally who distrusts the anonymous messages.

### Viktor Hale
Former executive connected to the predictive-risk project. Publicly respectable, privately determined to prevent the archive from resurfacing.

## Character rule
These facts are now working canon. Changes require an explicit continuity update.


### Mark Reid
Former project insider and witness to the buried PR-06 history. He agrees to provide evidence about the old authorization and parameter change, then disappears before the group can establish why. His testimony remains limited to what he personally witnessed.

### Thomas Rain
Former subject of the predictive-risk project and a direct human consequence of its behavioural intervention. He kept contemporaneous notes documenting calls, route changes and his own reactions. He refuses to let Alexei or the investigation turn his experience into a simplified narrative.

## Character continuity rules
- Alexei's knowledge grows through verified evidence and his own recovered memories; he must not know later revelations before their narrative discovery.
- Elena remains an independent investigator, not an omniscient guide or hidden controller.
- Daniel remains the technical verification partner and does not become the source of unverified conclusions.
- Sofia retains independent agency; Alexei may protect her, but does not decide her participation for her.
- Hale can apply pressure and possess project knowledge, but his claims remain claims until independently verified.
- Mark's witness knowledge is limited to his own experience and records.
- Thomas's account is personal testimony and contemporaneous documentation, not proof of every later event.
- M does not replace human character agency; its messages create conditions and pressure, while characters still make consequential choices.
