# CHAPTER_OUTLINE.md

# Book 1 — «Последнее сообщение»
Status: LOCKED WORKING OUTLINE

Target: ~40 chapters + prologue. Short, purposeful chapters. The exact word count will follow the story, not filler.

## PROLOGUE — The deletion
A former project insider discovers that the archive deletion protocol has started. He cannot stop it. He triggers the dormant communication protocol "M" and leaves one instruction: reach Alexei before Day 30.

## ACT I — THE WARNING

### Chapter 1 — 30 Days
Alexei receives the anonymous warning. He treats it as a scam but notices an unusual technical signature.

### Chapter 2 — Tomorrow
A second message arrives with a photograph apparently timestamped for the following day. Alexei is in the frame beside an unknown woman.

### Chapter 3 — The Frame
Alexei studies the image and finds details that make a simple fake unlikely. One tiny detail suggests the image was prepared specifically for him.

### Chapter 4 — Daniel
Alexei contacts former colleague Daniel Weiss. Daniel finds evidence of delayed transmission and manipulated metadata but cannot explain the physical scene.

### Chapter 5 — The Woman
Alexei identifies the woman as journalist Elena Varga. Her name triggers a fragment of memory he cannot place.

### Chapter 6 — Six Years
A reference to the old predictive-risk project surfaces. Alexei claims he left it six years ago and knows nothing important remains.

### Chapter 7 — The First Contradiction
Daniel discovers that one of Alexei's archived credentials was used after Alexei supposedly left the project.

### Chapter 8 — The Meeting
Alexei meets Elena. She knows details about the photograph that she should not know. She refuses to explain how she got them.

### Chapter 9 — Day 1
Alexei realizes the 30-day clock is deliberate. The story moves from harassment to investigation.

## ACT II — THE INVESTIGATION

### Chapter 10 — The Archive
Daniel traces a surviving project archive and finds a deletion schedule ending on Day 30.

### Chapter 11 — Predictive Risk
The old project is revealed as a behavioural prediction system used to model human decisions.

### Chapter 12 — The Missing Layer
A key modelling component is missing. Its author is listed under Alexei's old credentials.

### Chapter 13 — Sofia
Alexei's sister Sofia confronts him about the parts of his past he never discusses.

### Chapter 14 — The Second Photograph
"M" sends another image. This one appears to predict a conversation that has not happened yet.

### Chapter 15 — The Trap
Alexei follows the apparent prediction and discovers that the message was steering his behaviour.

### Chapter 16 — Elena's File
Elena shows Alexei evidence that the project was used beyond its declared purpose.

### Chapter 17 — The First Lie
Alexei discovers he once signed an internal authorization that he does not remember.

### Chapter 18 — M
The communication system is identified as an automated protocol built from archived project data and human-authored instructions.

### Chapter 19 — Not the Future
Daniel proves that the system cannot actually see the future. The apparent predictions rely on schedules, behavioural modelling and controlled releases.

### Chapter 20 — The Photograph Was Made
Alexei learns the famous "tomorrow" photograph was staged in advance. The emotional impact remains: someone knew exactly how he would react.

### Chapter 21 — The Other Alexei
A recovered file shows that Alexei's role in the project was substantially larger than he admitted.

### Chapter 22 — Elena's Question
Elena asks the question Alexei has avoided: what exactly happened six years ago?

## ACT III — THE CONVERGENCE

### Chapter 23 — The Incident
The buried incident is reconstructed. A prediction made by the project influenced a real-world decision with serious consequences.

### Chapter 24 — The Witness
A former insider agrees to testify but disappears before the meeting.

### Chapter 25 — Pressure
Viktor Hale enters directly. He does not threaten Alexei openly; he offers him a way to make the past disappear.

### Chapter 26 — Sofia Knows
Sofia finds an old encrypted backup and realizes Alexei has been protecting more than himself.

### Chapter 27 — The Human Cost
Alexei confronts the person harmed by the old project. The investigation becomes personal.

### Chapter 28 — M's Last Instruction
"M" gives Alexei the location of a redundant archive copy but warns that following the instruction will expose his own role.

### Chapter 29 — The Choice
Alexei can preserve the evidence anonymously or expose himself as one of its creators. He chooses the latter.

### Chapter 30 — Day 27
The archive becomes the target of coordinated destruction. Daniel and Elena split the evidence across independent locations.

### Chapter 31 — Day 28
Alexei realizes the deletion protocol contains a verification condition: the evidence must be connected to an accountable human witness.

### Chapter 32 — Day 29
The group prepares a public release. Viktor attempts to discredit Alexei using his own history.

## ACT IV — THE TRUTH

### Chapter 33 — Day 30
The scheduled deletion begins. Alexei enters the original project environment while Daniel maintains external copies.

### Chapter 34 — The Archive
Alexei finds the final record: proof that he helped build the behavioural model and later tried to erase his connection to it.

### Chapter 35 — The Witness
Alexei records a complete statement accepting his own role. This is what gives the surviving evidence a credible chain of accountability.

### Chapter 36 — Release
The archive is released through independently verifiable copies. The deletion system destroys only the original controlled environment.

### Chapter 37 — Aftermath
The investigation becomes public. Elena publishes the story. Sofia and Alexei begin repairing their relationship.

### Chapter 38 — The Cost
Alexei faces professional and legal consequences. The book does not erase his responsibility just because he exposed the truth.

### Chapter 39 — The Last Message
Alexei receives one final message from "M": only a timestamp from the following day.

### Chapter 40 — Tomorrow
The timestamp is real but unexplained. Alexei does not chase it immediately. For the first time, he chooses what to do without being directed.

## Ending function
Book 1 resolves the archive, the 30-day deadline, M's purpose, the photograph mechanism and Alexei's role. The final timestamp creates a sequel door without making Book 1 incomplete.
