# MASTER PLOT

Status: LOCKED WORKING ARCHITECTURE

## Act I — THE WARNING
Alexei Morozov receives the 30-day warning. He assumes fraud until the second message contains a photograph apparently taken tomorrow. The image shows Alexei beside Elena Varga, whom he believes he barely knows.

He investigates the image and finds details that cannot be explained by a simple scam.

## Act II — THE INVESTIGATION
Alexei asks Daniel Weiss for help. The investigation leads back to a confidential predictive-risk project from six years earlier.

Elena is revealed as an investigative journalist pursuing the same project. She knows Alexei played a larger role than he remembers admitting.

The reader receives competing explanations:
- extortion;
- corporate surveillance;
- fabricated evidence;
- an insider trying to expose the project;
- or Alexei himself being manipulated.

## Act III — THE CONVERGENCE
The messages are revealed to come from "M", an automated protocol assembled from archived project data and human-authored instructions.

"M" is not predicting the future. It is exploiting known schedules, behavioural models, staged evidence and controlled information releases.

Alexei discovers that he helped design the project's behavioural prediction layer. His past is not that of an innocent outsider.

The 30-day deadline is the scheduled archive-deletion window.

## Act IV — THE TRUTH
Alexei and Elena reach the archive while former insiders attempt to destroy it.

The final reversal: the system was not designed merely to save the archive. It was designed to force Alexei to become an accountable witness. His choices during the 30 days determine whether the evidence becomes credible.

Alexei exposes his own role alongside the project's abuses.

## Ending
The archive survives in independently verifiable copies. The public learns enough to understand the scandal, but several questions remain about how much of the original project was used elsewhere.

Final emotional beat: Alexei receives one last message. It contains no threat and no instruction — only a timestamp from the day after the archive survived.

This leaves a controlled opening for a second novel without invalidating the first.
