## Initial premise
An ordinary man receives an anonymous message:

> «У тебя осталось 30 дней. Не доверяй человеку, который придёт первым.»

Hours later he receives a photograph apparently taken tomorrow. He is in it, beside a stranger he has never seen.

The central mystery concerns who sends the messages, how the future photograph is possible, why the protagonist was selected, and what the evidence says about the protagonist himself.
