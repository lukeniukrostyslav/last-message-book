# TWISTS

Status: LOCKED WORKING ARCHITECTURE

## Twist 1 — The photograph
It looks like evidence from tomorrow. It is actually a controlled future-looking artifact released through delayed transmission and manipulated metadata.

## Twist 2 — Elena
Alexei initially treats Elena as the stranger from the photograph. She is not an assassin or supernatural messenger; she is investigating the same buried project.

## Twist 3 — The messages
"M" is not a person typing in real time. It is an automated protocol assembled from archived data and human instructions.

## Twist 4 — Alexei's past
Alexei was not merely a victim of the predictive-risk project. He helped build a key behavioural modelling component.

## Twist 5 — The 30 days
The deadline is not primarily about Alexei's death. It is the final deletion window for the archive.

## Final twist
The protocol's deeper purpose is to make Alexei an accountable witness. It needs his informed choices and testimony to make the surviving evidence credible.

## Foreshadowing requirements
Every twist must have visible clues before its reveal. Clues should be interpretable in more than one way but must have a fair solution after the reveal.
