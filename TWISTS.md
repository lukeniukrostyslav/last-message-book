# TWISTS

Status: DESIGN IN PROGRESS

## Required twist architecture
The story needs:
1. early mystery hook
2. first plausible explanation
3. contradiction that breaks it
4. deeper explanation
5. major reversal
6. final recontextualization

## Current protected idea
The ending should make the reader reconsider who the protagonist really is in relation to the central mystery.

## Anti-cheat rule
A twist must be supported by clues that exist before the reveal. After the reveal, earlier scenes should become more meaningful rather than simply invalid.
