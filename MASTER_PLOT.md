# MASTER PLOT

Status: DESIGN IN PROGRESS

## Act I — The Warning
Purpose:
- establish ordinary life
- deliver the 30-day warning
- make the second message impossible to dismiss
- introduce the photograph and first unknown person
- force the protagonist to investigate

## Act II — The Investigation
Purpose:
- competing explanations
- real clues mixed with misleading evidence
- relationships become unstable
- protagonist discovers that parts of his own history are unreliable or incomplete
- countdown creates escalating pressure

## Act III — The Convergence
Purpose:
- reveal how the evidence was produced
- connect the protagonist's past to the current threat
- expose the purpose of the 30-day deadline
- force a choice that cannot be undone

## Act IV — The Truth
Purpose:
- reveal the central identity/role twist
- recontextualize earlier clues
- resolve the main mystery without cheating
- leave only deliberately chosen sequel possibilities

## Important
This is structural scaffolding, not final plot. Specific events must be designed before chapters are drafted.
